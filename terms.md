## Purpose and permitted use

Personal Finance Hub is a private, non-commercial tool operated by its owner solely to retrieve and store information about the owner's own bank accounts. It is not offered to other users or businesses.

The application uses Enable Banking for account information access, following the owner's explicit authorization through the provider and bank. The application does not initiate payments or transfers and does not collect or store bank login passwords.

## Data and access

The owner chooses which accounts to authorize and remains responsible for protecting the local computer, application keys and stored records. Access can expire or be revoked. Revoking access stops future retrieval but does not automatically delete records already stored locally or in backups.

The bank's and Enable Banking's terms apply separately to their services. These terms do not grant the application any access beyond the consent given through those services.

## Availability and accuracy

This is an experimental personal tool. Synchronization may fail, be delayed or return incomplete data. The owner should check the bank's own records before relying on balances or transactions. There is no uptime or completeness commitment.
